Detailed Description

Handles touch gestures (drag-to-rotate, pinch-to-scale) and mouse fallback to transform the active patent model.

Smoothly interpolates towards targetRotation and targetScale each frame. Reacts to ScalingModeController.modeChanged, ModelTransformManager.OnLockModel, and ObjectSpawnerAR.objectSpawnedEvent.
